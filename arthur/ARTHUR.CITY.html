↓ Mostly Harmless ↓

🫖 Name

ARTHUR DENT'S — The house that was demolished. The planet that was demolished. The man who survived both in a bathrobe.

📋 Fact

Arthur Philip Dent. English. Unremarkable. Liked tea, Thursdays, and not being demolished. Lost all three. Found the universe instead. Never asked for it. Still complaining.

🎯 Target

To prove that the most ordinary man, armed with nothing but a towel and mild irritation, can survive the most extraordinary universe. And still want tea.

🫖 The 9 Rooms of Arthur Dent's

🏠
THE COTTAGE
Small. English. Thatched roof. Demolished on a Thursday for a bypass. The bypass was also demolished — for a hyperspace bypass.
"This must be Thursday. I never could get the hang of Thursdays."
🥿
THE BATHROBE
Burgundy terrycloth. Worn across 8 galaxies, 4 dimensions, and 2 apocalypses. Never washed. Still the most dignified garment in any room.
"He wore it to the end of the universe. It wore better than the universe."
🫖
THE KITCHEN
All he wanted. A proper cup of tea. The Nutrimatic Drinks Dispenser gave him something almost, but not quite, entirely unlike tea. Every time.
"He had been to the far reaches of the galaxy. None of them had a kettle."
🧬
THE BRAIN
Contains the Question to the Ultimate Answer. The Answer is 42. The Question was in his brain all along. He never checked.
"The Answer is 42. The Question is in the head of a man who can't find his slippers."
🚀
THE HEART OF GOLD
Stolen by Zaphod Beeblebrox. Powered by Infinite Improbability. The ship that runs on how unlikely things are. Arthur hated every second.
"The ship could do anything. Arthur wanted it to make tea. It couldn't."
📖
THE GUIDE
The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. More popular than the Encyclopaedia Galactica. Cheaper. And it has DON'T PANIC on the cover in large, friendly letters.
"In many of the more relaxed civilizations, a towel is about the most massively useful thing."
🍺
THE PUB
The Horse and Groom. Where Ford Prefect told Arthur the world was ending. Arthur's last pint on Earth. He didn't finish it.
"Ford, you're turning into a penguin. Stop it."
🌍
THE EARTH
Third planet from the sun. Mostly harmless. Actually: a supercomputer designed by mice to find the Ultimate Question. Demolished by Vogons for a hyperspace bypass. Paperwork was on display in Alpha Centauri.
"The plans were on display in the bottom of a locked filing cabinet stuck in a disused lavatory with a sign saying Beware of the Leopard."
🛁
THE BATHROOM
Where Arthur was lying when the Vogons came. In the bath. On a Thursday. About to lose everything. He put on his bathrobe. He never took it off.
"The universe is a big place. Arthur's bathroom was not. He preferred the bathroom."

👥 The Companions

🍺
FORD PREFECT
Not from Guildford. From Betelgeuse. Researcher for the Guide. Named himself after a car thinking they were the dominant species. Saved Arthur. Mostly by accident.
"Time is an illusion. Lunchtime doubly so."
🤪
ZAPHOD BEEBLEBROX
Two heads. Three arms. President of the Galaxy. Stole the Heart of Gold on his first day. Invented the Pan Galactic Gargle Blaster. Worst best friend in the universe.
"If there's anything more important than my ego, I want it caught and shot now."
💫
TRILLIAN
Tricia McMillan. Astrophysicist. Left Earth with Zaphod at a party in Islington. The one who got away. Arthur met her first. Of course he did.
"She left Earth voluntarily. Arthur still can't believe it."
🤖
MARVIN
The Paranoid Android. Brain the size of a planet. Asked to open doors and pick up paper. 50 billion times more intelligent than everyone. 50 billion times more depressed.
"Here I am, brain the size of a planet, and they ask me to pick up a piece of paper."
