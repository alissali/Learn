🚀 THE DEPARTURE

Space Station Omega — Year 2242

The Last Address Before the Stars · 16th Civilization · The One That Carries All the Others

🔥 Mordor 🏛️ Yabroud ☀️ Egypt 🦅 Aztec 🏛️ Greece 🐉 China ☸️ India 🌌 Aboriginal ❄️ Inuit 🗽 New York ✈️ Brasília 🛡️ Zulu ⚡ Viking 🗼 Tokyo 👑 London 🚀 Departure

They painted on cave walls for 65,000 years. They built pyramids. Temples. Igloos. Skyscrapers. They invented the wheel, the number zero, democracy, the internet, and the recipe for tea.

They fought over land, then seas, then air. They mapped the stars while killing each other over lines in the dirt.

And then — on a Tuesday in 2242 — they left.

Not because the Earth was dying. Because the question was dying.
"What Am I Doing Here" needed a bigger stage.

📸 The Station

🚀 The 9 Decks of Station Omega

🌍OBSERVATION DECK

360° view of Earth. The last window. Every departing soul stands here once.

"They all look back. Every single one. Then they turn around."

🧠THE BRIDGE

Holographic star charts. 12 officers. 144 destinations mapped. The decision is always human.

"The AI plots the course. A human says go. That's the deal."

🌳THE BIODOME

Real trees. Real rain. Real birds. 200 species from Earth. The last garden before the void.

"They brought the garden. The garden was always the point."

🔧HANGAR BAY

300 ships. From scout pods to colony arks. Every engine built from every civilization's knowledge.

"Chinese steel. Indian math. German engineering. African patience. One ship."

📚THE ARCHIVE

Every book. Every song. Every painting. Every recipe. Every prayer. Stored in crystal. Indestructible.

"They couldn't take the planet. So they took everything the planet ever said."

🍜THE GALLEY

800 cuisines from 16 civilizations. Sushi next to curry next to baguette next to injera. No borders.

"The menu has no countries. Only flavors."

⚕️MEDICAL BAY

Zero gravity surgery. Penicillin to nanobots. Every cure humanity ever found, plus the ones they're finding.

"They cured death. Then realized they still needed to cure loneliness."

🎓THE ACADEMY

Children learn every civilization. Aboriginal songlines on Monday. Greek philosophy on Tuesday. Zulu discipline on Wednesday.

"The curriculum is 65,000 years long. They start at age 5."

🚪GATE ZERO

The airlock. The final threshold. One step: everything you know is behind you. Everything you don't is ahead.

"The last thing they hear is the hiss. The first thing they see is forever."
