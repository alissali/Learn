ATHENS

CITY OF WISDOM
Est. ~3000 BCE · Where thought became architecture

🏛️ THE 9 DISTRICTS

🏛️

THE ACROPOLIS

Sacred rock · 156m altitude · Parthenon: 46 Doric columns · 12m Athena statue inside (gold + ivory) · Built in 15 years
"They put the most expensive thing in the city on top of a rock. Because beauty should be inconvenient."
🏺

THE AGORA

Market + court + democracy floor · Stoa of Attalos: 2 stories, 42 shops · Voting by pottery shard (ostracism) · Open to all free men
"Where you bought olives and overthrew a tyrant in the same afternoon."
🎭

THEATRE OF DIONYSUS

17,000 seats · Birthplace of tragedy and comedy · Sophocles, Euripides, Aristophanes all premiered here · Wine included
"They invented drama. Then they sat 17,000 people down and made them feel things."
📜

THE ACADEMY

Plato's school · 387 BCE · Lasted 916 years · Curriculum: mathematics, dialectic, astronomy · Entrance exam: geometry
"Let no one ignorant of geometry enter. The first admission requirement in history."
⚖️

THE PNYX

Assembly hill · 6,000 citizens voted · Speakers limited to water-clock time · Invented filibuster AND the fix for it
"Democracy was born on a hill. It's been uphill ever since."
⚓

PIRAEUS — THE PORT

400 triremes · 3 harbors · Long Walls connected port to city (6km) · Trade: grain, wine, pottery, slaves
"The sea paid for the Parthenon. Every column is a trade route."
🏟️

THE GYMNASIUM

3 gymnasia: Academy, Lyceum, Cynosarges · Training: wrestling, boxing, javelin · Philosophers taught between bouts · Nude (gymnos = naked)
"They debated the nature of reality. Naked. Covered in olive oil."
🏥

TEMPLE OF ASCLEPIUS

Healing sanctuary · Patients slept in temple (incubation) · Sacred snakes · Hippocrates trained here · First "Do No Harm"
"The prescription was: sleep in a temple full of snakes. And it worked."
🔥

THE KERAMEIKOS

Cemetery + pottery quarter · Sacred Way to Eleusis · War dead honored here · Finest black-figure pottery · 1,200 workshops
"Where the dead and the potters share the same clay."
