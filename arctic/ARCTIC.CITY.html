🗺️ The 9 Countries of the Ice

🏔️

THE IGLOO CITY

Spiral-cut snow blocks. Warmer inside than out by 40°C. Built in 3 hours. Architecture from water.

"They built houses from the thing that was trying to kill them."

🐋

WHALE GROUNDS

Bowhead whales hunted from kayaks. One whale feeds a village for a year. Every part used. Zero waste.

"The whale gives itself. You earn that."

🛷

DOG SLED TRAILS

14 dogs per sled. 100 km/day. The first highway system — written in ice, read by the dogs.

"The dogs know the way. The human knows when to listen."

🌊

KAYAK WATERS

Invented the kayak. Seal-skin over driftwood. Rolls in waves. 4,000 years before fibreglass.

"They made a boat that refuses to drown."

🔥

QULLIQ HEARTH

Seal-oil lamp. Burns for 24 hours. Heats, cooks, melts ice, dries clothes. One tool. Everything.

"One lamp. That's what stood between them and eternity."

❄️

ICE FISHING GROUNDS

Holes through 2m ice. Patience measured in hours. Catch: Arctic char, trout, seal. Zero grocery stores.

"The fish decides. You wait."

🌌

AURORA GROUNDS

Northern Lights = spirits playing football with a walrus skull. Not a metaphor. That's the explanation.

"The dead are playing. Don't whistle — they'll come down."

🦴

CARVING QUARTERS

Soapstone, bone, ivory, antler. Every piece tells a story. Sold to the world. The original NFT.

"The stone already knows what it is. They just remove the extra."

🎵

THROAT SINGING CIRCLE

Two women face to face. Breathing into each other. Creating sounds no instrument can make. The original beatbox.

"The song lives between two mouths."

🐻‍❄️ The 6 Spirits of the Ice

🐻‍❄️ NANUK — The Polar Bear

Master of Bears · Decides who hunts

The greatest hunter hunts the greatest prey. Mutual respect. Kill wrong, bears leave forever.

🌊 SEDNA — Mother of the Sea

Goddess of Ocean · Controls all sea life

Her fingers became seals, walruses, whales. When disrespected, she tangles her hair and the animals hide.

🌙 ALIGNAK — The Moon Spirit

Controller of Tides · Weather · Animals

Lives on the moon. Watches hunters. Rewards the respectful. The tides are his breathing.

💨 SILA — The Air Spirit

Weather · Breath · Consciousness

Not a god. The air itself. Thinking. Breathing. When Sila is angry, the blizzard speaks.

🦅 TULUQAQ — The Raven

Trickster · Creator · Brought Light

Stole the sun from a bag. Released it. The world got light. The Raven got nothing. Classic.

🐺 AMAROK — The Giant Wolf

Hunter of the Solitary · Teacher

Hunts those who hunt alone at night. Not punishment — lesson. The pack survives. The loner feeds the wolf.
