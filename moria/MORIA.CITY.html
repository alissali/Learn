THE GATES OF DURIN

SPEAK FRIEND AND ENTER

[ CLICK TO REVEAL — THE DOORS OF DURIN ]

The doors were beautiful once. Made in friendship between Elves and Dwarves — Celebrimbor's ithildin and Narvi's stone. The inscription was not a riddle. It was a welcome. The word was Mellon. Friend. The simplest answer. The hardest to remember.

"It's a riddle. What's the Elvish word for friend?"

🏛️

DWARROWDELF

THE GREAT HALL — A THOUSAND PILLARS IN THE DARK

[ CLICK TO REVEAL — THE ENDLESS PILLARS ]

They called it Dwarrowdelf. The Mansion of the Dwarves. A hall so vast that torchlight could not find the ceiling. Every pillar carved by hand. Every surface a testament to what happens when a people pour their entire soul into stone. It took a thousand years. It was abandoned in a single night.

"There's an eye-opener, and no mistake."

⚒️

THE FORGE

WHERE MITHRIL WAS SHAPED INTO LEGEND

[ CLICK TO REVEAL — THE FORGE STILL GLOWS ]

The forges of Khazad-dûm were the greatest in Middle-earth. Here they worked mithril — truesilver — lighter than silk and harder than dragon scales. A single mithril coat was worth more than the entire Shire. Bilbo wore one. He never understood what he was wearing. The dwarves who made it would have appreciated that.

"That was a kingly gift. I never told him, but its worth was greater than the value of the Shire."

THEY ARE COMING
📖

THE CHAMBER OF MAZARBUL

BALIN'S TOMB — WE CANNOT GET OUT

[ CLICK TO REVEAL — THE BOOK OF RECORDS ]

Here lies Balin, Son of Fundin, Lord of Moria. He came back to reclaim the kingdom. He succeeded for five years. Then the drums began. The last entry in the Book of Records: "We cannot get out. We cannot get out. They have taken the bridge and Second Hall. The end comes soon. Drums, drums in the deep. They are coming."

"We cannot get out. The end comes. Drums, drums in the deep."

🔥

THE BRIDGE

YOU SHALL NOT PASS

[ CLICK TO REVEAL — THE BALROG COMES ]

A Balrog. A demon of the ancient world. Fire and shadow. It had slept beneath the mountain since the First Age, waiting. The dwarves woke it. And on a bridge fifty feet long with no railing above a bottomless abyss, a wizard who looked like an old man said the only thing there was to say.

"You cannot pass! I am a servant of the Secret Fire, wielder of the flame of Anor. The dark fire will not avail you, flame of Udûn. Go back to the Shadow! YOU SHALL NOT PASS!"

💎

THE MITHRIL TREASURY

THE WEALTH THAT DESTROYED THEM

[ CLICK TO REVEAL — VEINS OF TRUESILVER ]

Mithril. Truesilver. Found nowhere else in Middle-earth. The veins ran deep — deeper than the dwarves should have followed. But greed is not a decision. It is gravity. You fall into it. And the deeper you dig, the darker it gets, and the brighter the silver shines, and you cannot stop.

"Moria! Moria! Wonder of the Northern world! Too deep we delved there, and woke the nameless fear."

THE AGES OF MORIA

⛏️ THE FOUNDING
Durin the Deathless wakes at Mount Gundabad. Walks south. Finds the Mirrormere. Sees a crown of stars. Begins to dig.
🏛️ THE GOLDEN AGE
Mithril discovered. The forges blaze. Dwarrowdelf rises pillar by pillar. Narvi and Celebrimbor build the Doors. Dwarf and Elf as friends. It would not last.
💎 THE DEEP YEARS
They delve deeper. The mithril veins pull them down. The treasury overflows. The kingdom stretches beneath three mountains. The greatest civilization under the earth.
🔥 DURIN'S BANE
Year 1980 of the Third Age. They dig too deep. They wake Durin's Bane — a Balrog of Morgoth. Durin VI is killed. His son Náin I falls the next year. Moria is abandoned in a single generation.
📖 BALIN'S RETURN
Year 2989. Balin leads a company back. Five years of hope. Then orcs. Then drums. Then silence. The Book of Records ends mid-sentence.
🧙 THE FELLOWSHIP
Year 3019. Nine walkers enter. Eight walk out. Gandalf falls with the Balrog. From the lowest dungeon to the highest peak. He was sent back.
⛏️ THE RECLAMATION
Fourth Age. The Balrog is dead. The orcs are scattered. Durin VII, the Last, leads the Longbeards home. Moria is reclaimed. The hammers ring again.
