🗺️ The Nine Neighborhoods

🏡BAG END — Hobbiton
The finest hobbit hole in the Shire. Built by Bungo Baggins. Inherited by Bilbo. Inherited by Frodo. One ring passed through that round green door and changed everything.
"In a hole in the ground there lived a hobbit. Not a nasty, dirty, wet hole. A very comfortable hole."
🍺THE GREEN DRAGON — Bywater
The finest pub in the four farthings. Ale brewed on site. Gossip brewed faster. Sam's favorite. Where adventures are discussed but never desired.
"You can drink your fancy ales. You can drink them by the flagon. But the only brew for the brave and true comes from the Green Dragon."
🌾FARMER MAGGOT'S FIELDS — Marish
Mushrooms. The best in the Shire. Hobbits have been stealing them for generations. Maggot has three dogs. The dogs have won every argument.
"Short cuts make long delays. But mushrooms make everything forgivable."
🌳THE PARTY TREE — Hobbiton Hill
The great oak under which Bilbo gave his 111th birthday speech. Disappeared in a flash. The tree stayed. The tree always stays.
"I don't know half of you half as well as I should like, and I like less than half of you half as well as you deserve."
📬THE POST OFFICE — Michel Delving
Capital of the Shire. Home of the Mayor. The Mayor's primary duty: presiding over banquets. Government as it should be.
"The Mayor's job is to eat. The Shire's finest constitutional achievement."
🌻SAM'S GARDEN — 3 Bagshot Row
The most important garden in Middle-earth. Tended by Samwise Gamgee. He replanted the Shire with Galadriel's gift. The real hero's real weapon: a box of dirt and a silver seed.
"I can't carry it for you. But I can carry you."
🍄BUCKLAND — East of the Brandywine
Where the wild hobbits live. East of the river. They swim. SWIM. Other hobbits consider this suspicious at best, unnatural at worst.
"They're queer folk in Buckland. They do things differently there. They swim."
🧙GANDALF'S CART — The Road
He arrives precisely when he means to. The cart is full of fireworks. The fireworks are full of magic. The magic is full of trouble. The trouble is full of wonder.
"A wizard is never late. Nor is he early. He arrives precisely when he means to."
🛤️THE ROAD — Going Ever On
It starts at Bag End. It doesn't end. Every hobbit who stepped on it came back changed. Some didn't come back at all. But the road remembers all of them.
"The Road goes ever on and on, down from the door where it began."

🧑‍🌾 The Six Hobbits Who Changed Everything

💍
BILBO BAGGINS
Found a ring. Wrote a book. Gave a speech. Disappeared. Lived to 131. The first hobbit to prove that small feet can walk far.
🌋
FRODO BAGGINS
Carried the Ring to Mount Doom. The weight broke him. He saved the world and couldn't stay in it. The wound that never healed.
🌱
SAMWISE GAMGEE
Gardener. Ring-bearer. The real hero. Carried Frodo up the mountain. Then went home and planted a tree. Mayor for 49 years. Father of 13.
🗡️
MERRY BRANDYBUCK
Helped kill the Witch-king. Came home. Became Master of Buckland. Proved that courage comes in all sizes.
🛡️
PIPPIN TOOK
Fool of a Took. Guard of the Citadel. Looked into the palantír. Survived. The youngest, the bravest, the hungriest.
🧙
GANDALF
Not a hobbit. But the Shire was his favorite place in Middle-earth. He chose hobbits to save the world. He was right.

🧭 The Shire's Place in the Arc
