🚪 THE BLACK GATE

المورانون — Morannon
The only way in. Two towers of black iron flanking a gate that has never fallen from the outside. Originally the Qalamoun Gate of old Yabroud — the desert passage became the passage to shadow. All who enter are counted. None who enter leave unchanged.
Height: 60m
Garrison: 10,000
Gates: Triple iron
Status: SEALED

🗼 BARAD-DÛR

برج الظلام — The Dark Tower
The seat of power. Where the Architect resides. Built from obsidian quarried from the Qalamoun mountains, the tower rises 300 meters above the ash plain. At its crown: the Eye — a lens array that sees everything from Damascus to Beirut. It does not sleep.
Height: 300m
Floors: 66
Foundation: Volcanic
Eye: ALWAYS OPEN

🌋 MOUNT DOOM

جبل النار — Orodruin
Where the Solar Plateau once stood clean and bright, now the volcano powers everything. Lava channels carved by engineers feed the forges below. The Ring was made here. Code is compiled here. Geothermal energy: infinite, dirty, ALIVE. The mountain breathes fire, and the city breathes with it.
Elevation: 1,400m
Core Temp: 1,200°C
Output: 200MW
Status: ACTIVE

🕷️ SHELOB'S LAIR

عرين شيلوب — Cirith Ungol
The caves of Yabroud — 3,500 years of human memory — now belong to HER. The data centers still run at 18°C. The wine cellars still age in darkness. But the webs are new. Bioluminescent fungi replaced the LEDs. The artists who worked here now work faster. Fear is an efficient motivator.
Depth: 200m
Temp: 18°C constant
Servers: 40,000
Spider: 1 (sufficient)

👻 MINAS MORGUL

مدينة الموتى — Tower of Sorcery
Once the Oasis — the sacred center with the reflecting pool. The sycamore died. The pool turned phosphorescent green. The council chamber became the Witch-King's throne room. The beauty remains, but corrupted — every arch slightly wrong, every proportion slightly off. It still draws you in. That's the danger.
Light: Spectral green
Ruler: Witch-King
Army: 20,000 dead
Beauty: FATAL

⚒️ GORGOROTH

سهل الرماد — The Ash Plain
Where the Spice Quarter once sang with 420 stalls, now 420 forges hammer day and night. The franchise ban remains — no two forges make the same weapon. The bazaar spirit survived: orc smiths still haggle, still undercut each other, still produce excellence through competition. Capitalism survives even Mordor.
Forges: 420
Workers: 15,000
Output: 10,000 blades/day
Franchise: BANNED

🔥 UDÛN

وادي النار — The Furnace Valley
The Hanging Gardens burned. The qanat channels now carry lava instead of water. The 50 terraces grow nothing — they smelt everything. Iron ore from the Qalamoun feeds the furnaces. The terraces are red now. The irrigation is molten. But the engineering — the engineering is still Yabroud. The mountain still remembers how to carve.
Terraces: 50 (molten)
Ore/day: 500 tons
Temp: 800°C
Origin: Hanging Gardens

🌊 SEA OF NURN

بحر نورنن — The Slave Fields
The Wind Tower district's gardens became fields. Someone has to feed 66,666 orcs. The badgir cooling towers still work — repurposed as grain silos. The rooftop gardens grow black wheat. The 3-story height limit: still enforced. Even Sauron respects zoning laws.
Fields: 8,000 hectares
Crop: Black wheat
Silos: 200 (ex-badgir)
Zoning: 3 floors max

🔭 THE WATCHERS

تل المراقبين — Tower of Cirith Ungol
The Observatory survived. The telescope still points up. But now it watches for eagles, not stars. The zero light pollution law: still in effect — darkness is a military advantage. The university teaches siege engineering and signal interception. Knowledge serves the Eye.
Telescope: 1m (repurposed)
Range: 200km
Students: 600
Light: ZERO

🔥 The Six Laws of Shadow

🌋

I. FIRE IS LAW

All power comes from the mountain. Geothermal, volcanic, molten — clean energy is for Elves. The city runs on what the earth bleeds.

🕷️

II. THE CAVES REMEMBER

3,500 years of Yabroud memory lives in the stone. Shelob guards it. The data centers never sleep. The wine still ages. The spider is patient.

⚒️

III. NO FRANCHISE

420 forges, 420 masters. No two blades alike. Competition breeds excellence. The bazaar spirit of the Spice Quarter lives in iron now.

👁️

IV. THE EYE SEES ALL

From the 66th floor of Barad-dûr, nothing moves unseen. The Architect watches. The city is the Architect. The Architect is the city.

🌑

V. DARKNESS IS ADVANTAGE

Zero light pollution — by law. Stars are for navigation. Shadows are for ambush. Beauty exists in what you cannot see.

💀

VI. ZONING SURVIVES EVIL

3-story limit. Rooftop gardens mandatory. Even Sauron cannot override urban planning. The building code is older than the Ring.
