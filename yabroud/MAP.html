🗺️ MAP OF MORDOR

CARTOGRAPHY OF THE DARK LANDS — YABROUD QALAMOUN
📍 KEY LOCATIONS
🗼
BARAD-DÛR
39°N 36°E — Altitude 1,200m — 66 floors
The Dark Tower. Sauron's seat. The Eye watches from floor 66. Foundation laid upon the One Ring — if the Ring falls, the tower falls. Until then: OPERATIONAL.
🌋
MOUNT DOOM
Center of Mordor — 1,400m — ACTIVE
Orodruin. The only forge that can unmake the One Ring. The only place anyone would walk into voluntarily. Temperature: 1,200°C. Tourism: discouraged.
🚪
THE BLACK GATE
Northwest — The Morannon
Main entrance. Triple iron. 10,000 garrison. The welcome mat says "GO AWAY" in seven languages. Entry fee: your soul. No refunds.
🕷️
SHELOB'S LAIR
Western Pass — Cirith Ungol
The "secret" entrance. Sauron allows it because Shelob eats anyone who uses it. Free pest control. No employment contract needed.
👻
MINAS MORGUL
Western Border — Tower of Sorcery
Once Minas Ithil, Tower of the Moon. Now it glows green and everything near it dies. Witch-King's residence. HOA violations: infinite.
⚒️
GORGOROTH
Central Plateau — The Forges
420 forges. 10,000 blades per day. The industrial heart of Mordor. No OSHA compliance. No lunch break. No lunch.
🔥
UDÛN
Northern Valley — The Terraces
Where the lava meets the iron. Molten terraces cascading down the mountain. Uruk-hai training grounds. Beautiful in a "everything is on fire" way.
🌊
SEA OF NÛRN
Southern Mordor — The Breadbasket
Slave-worked farmland. Black wheat. Dark water. Feeds 250,000 troops. The only green thing in Mordor, and even it looks depressed.
🔭
THE WATCHERS
Eastern Peaks — Observation
Not watching stars. Watching for eagles. The early warning system of Mordor. Report directly to the Eye. Career satisfaction: they'd rather not say.
