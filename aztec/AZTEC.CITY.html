🏛️ THE 9 DISTRICTS

🔺

TEMPLO MAYOR

The Great Pyramid. Twin temples at the summit — Red for Huitzilopochtli (War), Blue for Tlaloc (Rain). 60m high. Rebuilt 7 times, each larger. The center of the universe. Literally — they measured.
"Two gods share one pyramid. They never argued about rent."
🦅

THE EAGLE PRECINCT

Warrior academy of the Eagle Knights. Only nobles. Training: combat, poetry, astronomy, sacrifice protocol. Graduation rate: 40%. The rest became sacrifices (efficient).
"You either wore the eagle — or you fed it."
🐆

THE JAGUAR HOUSE

Elite Jaguar Warriors. Full jaguar skin armor. Obsidian swords (macuahuitl) sharper than steel. Night raids. Zero mercy. The empire's teeth.
"The jaguar doesn't announce itself. Neither do we."
🏺

TLATELOLCO MARKET

Largest market in the Americas. 60,000 daily visitors. Cacao = currency. Gold, jade, feathers, obsidian, slaves, chocolate. Judges on-site. No haggling allowed (they lied — everyone haggled).
"60,000 people and not one receipt."
🌿

THE CHINAMPAS

Floating gardens. Artificial islands anchored by willow roots. Maize, squash, beans, flowers, chili. Fed 300,000 people. No tractors. No problem.
"We grew food on water. Europe was still arguing about crop rotation."
🌊

THE AQUEDUCT

Twin-pipe stone aqueduct from Chapultepec. 5km. Fresh water while one pipe cleaned, the other flowed. Redundancy: INVENTED HERE. 300,000 people, zero thirst.
"Two pipes. Because one would be ordinary."
💀

THE TZOMPANTLI

The Skull Rack. Thousands of skulls displayed on wooden frames. Not decoration — ACCOUNTING. Every skull: a debt paid to the sun. The sun demanded blood. The sun got blood.
"The sun doesn't ask politely."
🎭

THE POETRY QUARTER

Flower and Song (In Xochitl In Cuicatl). Poets had higher status than warriors. Poetry competitions at the palace. Nezahualcoyotl — philosopher-king. The empire wrote before it fought.
"The sword keeps the empire. The poem keeps the soul."
🌅

THE CAUSEWAY GATES

3 causeways connecting the island to the mainland. Drawbridges at each gate. Removable sections for defense. Cortés used them to enter. Then couldn't use them to leave. (La Noche Triste.)
"Welcome in. Good luck out."

⚡ THE PANTHEON

☀️

HUITZILOPOCHTLI

God of War & Sun
The Left-Handed Hummingbird. Born fully armed. Killed his 400 siblings at birth. Demands blood to keep the sun moving. Efficient management.
🐍

QUETZALCOATL

Feathered Serpent — Wind & Knowledge
Created humanity from bones and his own blood. Left, promising to return. Cortés arrived. Worst. Timing. Ever.
🌧️

TLALOC

God of Rain & Fertility
Lives in a mountain paradise. Controls rain, floods, drought. Demands child sacrifices. The children's tears = rain. Aztec logic: brutal but consistent.
💀

MICTLANTECUHTLI

Lord of the Dead
Rules Mictlan — the underworld. Skeleton with eyeballs. The dead walk 4 years to reach him. No shortcuts. Death is a commute.
🌙

COYOLXAUHQUI

Moon Goddess
Tried to kill her mother. Brother Huitzilopochtli dismembered her. Her body became the moon. Family dinner: CANCELLED.
🦅

TEZCATLIPOCA

Smoking Mirror — Fate & Night
Sees everything in his obsidian mirror. Rival of Quetzalcoatl. Tricked him with alcohol and a prostitute. Even gods have bad nights.

⚔️ THE ARMY OF THE FIFTH SUN

🦅

EAGLE WARRIORS — Cuāuhpilli

Elite noble order. Eagle-head helmets. Capture, don't kill — sacrifices need to be alive. Status: APEX. 4+ captures required for membership.
🐆

JAGUAR WARRIORS — Ocēlōpilli

Full jaguar skin. Night specialists. The macuahuitl (obsidian sword) could decapitate a horse. Cortés confirmed this personally.
🗡️

SHORN ONES — Cuachicqueh

Mohawk. Blue/red face paint. Vow: NEVER RETREAT. The most feared unit. When they arrived, the battle was already over.
🏹

ATLATL CORPS

Spear-throwers. 150m range. Faster reload than European crossbows. Projectile velocity: enough to pierce Spanish armor. Surprise.
💀

THE FLOWER WARS — Xochiyaoyotl

Scheduled wars for the sole purpose of capturing sacrifices. Both sides agreed on time and place. War as APPOINTMENT. The gods had a subscription.
