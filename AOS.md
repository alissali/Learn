## STRUCTURE — Five Levels

### Level 1 — OBJECTS (3)
Atomic carriers of Semantics.

| Object | Role |
|--------|------|
| NAME | Identity |
| FACT | Truth |
| TARGET | Direction |

Three Objects. No more. No less.
Together they form one complete unit of meaning.

---

### Level 2 — EXPRESSION
An Expression is composed of three Objects.
One NAME. One FACT. One TARGET.
One coherent unit of meaning. Indivisible.

---

### Level 3 — SENTENCE
A Sentence is composed of Expressions invoking Operations.
Executable meaning.
A Sentence does not describe — it acts.

---

### Level 4 — DICTIONARY
One Dictionary per User Discipline.
A Dictionary organizes Sentences and Operations for one domain.
It is the vocabulary of a discipline, complete and bounded.

**First Dictionary: JuX**
Discipline: Software.
Sentences: buy / sell / mortgage / roll / pay / move.
Operations: running on Ubuntu.

---

### Level 5 — SERVICE
What Users demand.
Services are realized by Operations.
The user sees the Service. The architecture runs the Operation.
