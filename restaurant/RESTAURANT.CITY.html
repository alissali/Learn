🍽️ THE 9 TABLES

🥂

TABLE 1 — THE RECEPTION

The maître d' has been expecting you. Since before you were born. Literally — time travel makes reservations easy.
"Your table is ready. It has been ready since the Big Bang."
🪟

TABLE 2 — THE WINDOW SEAT

Panoramic view of the cosmos collapsing. Galaxies spiral into nothing. Stars scream. You eat breadsticks.
"Best view in the house. Also the last."
🔥

TABLE 3 — THE KITCHEN

19 civilizations' cuisines. One kitchen. Chefs from every era cook the final meal. Damascus bread. Aboriginal fire. Tokyo precision. Aztec chocolate.
"Every dish on the menu was invented by someone who thought they had time."
🍸

TABLE 4 — THE BAR

Pan Galactic Gargle Blasters. Bottled supernovae. Melange on tap. The bartender remembers everyone's last order. Because it IS the last order.
"What'll it be? Choose carefully. The cellar expires in 17 minutes."
🎭

TABLE 5 — THE STAGE

Live entertainment while reality disintegrates. The house band plays the greatest hits of 65,000 years. Final song: chosen by the audience. Every time, they pick the same one.
"Ladies and gentlemen — the UNIVERSE! …One night only."
📜

TABLE 6 — THE BILL

Pay before the Big Bang, collect interest. One penny at the dawn of time = enough to cover dinner for the entire species. Including tip.
"The most expensive meal in history. Paid with the cheapest trick in physics."
🚪

TABLE 7 — THE EXIT

Nobody leaves. Not because they can't. Because where would you go? The universe is closing. Might as well have coffee.
"Check please. …Where exactly would you like to go?"
🧡

TABLE 8 — THE ARCHITECT'S TABLE

Reserved permanently. Center of the room. One orange on the table. One coffee. A notebook. He's still writing. The universe is ending and he's still writing.
"What am I doing here? …Having dinner. Obviously."
💫

TABLE 9 — THE LAST TABLE

The table that was set but nobody sat at. Every restaurant has one. A chair for the one who didn't make it. The empty seat is the most important one.
"Set a place for the absent. They built half of what you ate."
